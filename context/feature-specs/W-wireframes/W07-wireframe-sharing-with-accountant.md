# W07: Wireframe: Sharing With the Accountant

Wireframes for publishing a report to Google Sheets, sharing it, and reviewing the accountant's changes. This is the part of the app QuickBooks does not have, so the client should see it clearly.

## Depends On

- W06

## Implementation

1. Run `/wireframe` for "the Shared sheets page, sharing a sheet with the accountant as viewer, commenter or editor, and the Accountant changes page", choosing Wireframes only.

2. Check the options cover: the list of published sheets with who can see each and at what role, the share dialog showing the full email and role with a confirmation, changing a role, Stop sharing, and **Open in Google Sheets**.

3. Check the Accountant changes page shows each waiting change with the old and new value, and Accept or Reject, and makes clear that nothing in the books changes until accepted.

4. Record the option names and the recommendation in the progress file.

## Scope Limits

- No Google sign-in screens. The sign-in happens in the browser.

## Check When Done

- The wireframe opens and every tab works.
- The share confirmation and an accepted and a rejected change can be seen in the recommended option.
- Invented names and emails only, such as `accountant@example.com`.
