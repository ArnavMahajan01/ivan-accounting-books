# W06a: Wireframe: Customise a Report

Wireframes for changing how a report looks: adding or removing columns (last quarter, same quarter last year, % of income, month by month), showing, hiding and grouping rows, and saving a layout as his own report. Added on 2026-11-22: the author asked for reports he can shape, not one fixed profit and loss.

Part of the client review pack. It follows W06 and comes before the W10 meeting.

Note (2026-11-22): the author asked for his own new columns too. Options 2 and 3 now have "New column…": his notes, or a calculation picked from a list (difference, % change, % of income, GST in an amount, flag if over). Builds into spec 102.

## Depends On

- W06

## Implementation

1. Make 5 black-and-white options in `wireframe/2211-customise-a-report/` with the wireframe skill, Wireframes only.
2. Cover: columns he can add and remove; rows he can show, hide and group; saving and reopening his own layouts; ready-made layouts for someone who is not a numbers person.
3. Invented data only, matching W03 to W09.
4. Add it to `wireframe/index.html`, and a section to `context/wireframe-feedback.md`.

## Scope Limits

- Layout only. The figures always come from the books, so a customised report still balances.
- Free-form work (his own formulas, notes, extra rows) is W07a, in Google Sheets.

## Builds Into

- Specs 100 and 101 (and 102 for his own columns).

## Check When Done

- The wireframe opens from `wireframe/index.html` and every tab works.
- The author has clicked through all 5 options.
