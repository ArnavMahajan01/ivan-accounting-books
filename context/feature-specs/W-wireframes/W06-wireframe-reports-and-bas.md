# W06: Wireframe: Reports and BAS Worksheet

Wireframes for the reports list, one report (profit and loss), and the BAS worksheet with the period lock.

## Depends On

- W05

## Implementation

1. Run `/wireframe` for "the Reports page, a profit and loss report, and the BAS worksheet for a quarter", choosing Wireframes only.

2. Check the options cover: picking a date range or BAS quarter, the actions Export CSV, Export PDF and Publish to Google Sheets, opening the lines behind a figure, and locking the period once the BAS is done, with a confirmation.

3. The BAS worksheet shows the simpler BAS labels G1, 1A and 1B with invented figures. It must not look like it lodges anything with the ATO.

4. Record the option names and the recommendation in the progress file.

## Scope Limits

- No balance sheet or trial balance wireframe, unless the client asks. They follow the profit and loss layout.

## Check When Done

- The wireframe opens and every tab works.
- The lock confirmation can be seen in the recommended option.
- No wording suggests the app lodges the BAS.
