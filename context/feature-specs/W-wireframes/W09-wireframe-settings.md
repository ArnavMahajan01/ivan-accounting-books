# W09: Wireframe: Settings

Wireframes for Settings: business details, bank accounts, the Google connection, backups and appearance.

## Depends On

- W08, or W07 if W08 was skipped

## Implementation

1. Run `/wireframe` for "the Settings page with Business, Bank accounts, Google, Backups and Appearance sections", choosing Wireframes only.

2. Check the options cover: the GST basis and BAS frequency choices, a connected and a disconnected Google account, backup on or off with its last backup time and a **Restore** action with a warning, and the light, dark or system theme choice.

3. Record the option names and the recommendation in the progress file.

## Scope Limits

- No licensing or account settings. Those belong to the scale version (F02), and must not appear.

## Check When Done

- The wireframe opens and every tab works.
- The restore warning can be seen in the recommended option.
