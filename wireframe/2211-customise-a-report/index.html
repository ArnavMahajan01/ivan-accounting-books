Recommended: Option 2, Side Panel, starting from Option 5's ready layouts and saving into Option 4's "My reports". He sees the effect of each choice at once, and "+ New column…" lets him add his own notes or calculations. Anything more free-form (his own formulas, notes, extra rows) happens in Google Sheets: see "Working With the Google Sheet".
