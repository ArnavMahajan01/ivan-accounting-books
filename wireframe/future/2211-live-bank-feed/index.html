What happens

  • An Open Banking provider, accredited under the Consumer Data Right, reads your Westpac transactions each day.
  • It passes new lines to this app. They land in To review, the same as a file import.
  • The provider keeps a copy while your consent lasts, for up to 12 months. You can stop it at any time.
  • The app never sees your Westpac password.
