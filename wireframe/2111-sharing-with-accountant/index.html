Summary

OptionApproachClear control of access
1: Safe TablesA sheets table and a changes table★★★★☆
2: People FirstAccess grouped by person, role changed in the row★★★★★
3: Sheet CardsA Drive-like access card per sheet★★★☆☆
4: Change DiffEach change shown with its effect on the books★★★★☆
5: InboxOne page, changes on top, sheets below★★★☆☆

Recommended: Option 2, People First, with the "What accepting does" box from Option 4 added to each change. The risk that matters most here is someone keeping access they should not have. Listing access by person answers "what can my accountant see?" in one place, with one button to stop it all.

Questions for the client: which columns may the accountant edit (client question 7 assumes Account, GST and Note)? Is there anyone besides his accountant to share with? All names and emails are invented.
