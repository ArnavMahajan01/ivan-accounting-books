To review · 3 minutes

Review and accept bank lines

  1. Open To review in the sidebar. The number beside it is how many lines are waiting.
    Sample image: the To review table
  2. Check the suggested account on each line. "From your rule" means you chose it for this supplier before.
  3. Change it if needed: click the account and type a few letters, such as "mat" for Materials & supplies.
  4. Accept: press Enter for one line, or tick several and click Accept selected.

UX notes

  • ① Stars and his own tags filter the list, exactly as in the Admin Dashboard. Works offline.
  • ② "Keep on screen" turns the guide into a small panel that stays while he moves around the app (see Option 3).
