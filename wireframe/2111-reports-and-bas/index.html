Recommended: Option 3, Quarter Close. Locking a quarter cannot be undone in the app as planned, so it should come after the lines are reviewed and the accounts reconciled. The checklist makes that order visible. The other reports stay exactly as in Option 1.

Questions for the client: does he use cash or accrual GST (client question 3)? Should a locked quarter ever be unlockable, for example to fix a mistake the accountant finds? All figures are invented.
