UX notes

  • ① Updated 2026-11-22 from the author's QuickBooks screenshots: "Post", as in QuickBooks, rather than "Accept". Tick several lines and post them at once.
  • ② Spent and Received are separate columns, and From/To holds the payee, as in his QuickBooks "For review" tab.
  • ③ A line is either matched to something already in the books, such as an invoice, or given an account. Under it, in small words, why.
  • ④ A transfer is shown as a pair with the line in the other account. Click it, or Split…, to see their sheets.
  • ⑤ Every action has a key, shown at the bottom.

Summary

OptionApproachSpeed through 50 lines
1: Review TableSpreadsheet table like his QuickBooks tab: Spent, Received, From/To, Match or account, Post★★★★★
2: One at a TimeOne big line per screen, Enter for next★★★☆☆
3: GroupedLines grouped by suggested account, accept a group★★★★☆
4: Split ViewList on the left, full editor on the right★★★☆☆

Recommended: Option 1, Review Table. It is the fastest way through 50 lines: rows he can scan, many accepted at once, and every action on a key. It is also closest to the QuickBooks "For review" tab he already knows. The "Accept all suggested" button from Option 3 can sit in its toolbar.

Questions for the client: should a rule be saved automatically after he picks the same account twice, or only when he ticks a box (Option 4)? Does he ever split a line? All data is invented.
